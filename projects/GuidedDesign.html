Guided Bridge Design

CIV313 Reinforced Concrete I
Jan 2026 – May 2026
Co-contributor: Nedel Ellacer and Vanik Park

In this project for a team of three, I contributed to the complete structural design of a reinforced concrete bridge as part of CIV313 at the University of Toronto. The design process covered all major structural components — the girder, pier, and foundation — following the CIV313S 2026 Guided Design Specification and Canadian concrete design standards.

My responsibilities included calculating longitudinal bending demand and capacity and analyzing moment and shear envelopes along the girder span. For the pier and foundation, I contributed to constructing the interaction diagram for the beam-column and developing truss models to determine reinforcement steel requirements at the foundation and at the corner pier-to-girder connection.

The final design successfully satisfied all Ultimate Limit State (ULS) and Serviceability Limit State (SLS) load combinations, including dead load, live load, and seismic cases. Through this project, I strengthened my ability to apply structural theory to a realistic multi-component bridge system, interpret bending moment and shear envelopes, and make informed design decisions around steel curtailment and reinforcement detailing.
